About

Hi. I'm Yume.

I create expressive digital portraits that blend painterly realism with anime-inspired aesthetics — loose, confident brushwork wrapped around large, unflinching eyes.

My work explores emotion, colour harmony and cinematic lighting, often set against poster-like collage elements: torn typography, halftone dots, pixel colour blocks. Each piece starts from a real photo reference and is rebuilt, frame by frame, into something warmer and stranger.

Every portrait is a study in contrast — soft, kawaii surface detail against a gaze that gives very little away.

What's the turnaround time?+

Most portrait commissions take 1–3 weeks depending on the current queue and complexity. You'll get a sketch check-in before final rendering.

Do you accept commercial use requests?+

Yes — commercial and merchandise usage is available at the Commercial Illustration tier, with licensing terms agreed before work begins.

What references do you need?+

Clear, well-lit reference photos or a detailed written description, plus any mood-board images that capture the colours or feeling you want.

How do payments work?+

A non-refundable 50% deposit secures your slot, with the remainder due on completion, before final files are delivered.
